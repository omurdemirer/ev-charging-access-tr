# ev-charging-access-tr

Code and derived results for the study:

> Demirer, Ö. *Same stations, different inequality: opposing effects of service speed,
> energy-distance and queueing definitions on measured electric vehicle charging access.*
> Manuscript submitted for publication.

The study measures public EV charging accessibility for the whole of Türkiye (13,067 public
stations, 175,175 population origins, 969 districts) with a type-specific enhanced two-step
floating catchment area method and asks how sensitive measured inequality is to three
measurement definitions: service rate (quality), energy-distance (kWh instead of km,
direction-dependent) and congestion (immediate-service-weighted supply, Erlang-C). The change in
the population-weighted Gini coefficient is split across the three definitions with a Shapley
decomposition. A bi-objective mixed-integer model, built on the distance catchment, links the
results to investment.

Archived version: [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23037338](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23037338) ·
this release: **v1.1.1** (5 Oct 2026; v1.1.0 of 2 Oct 2026 plus a corrected centre budget share)

Author: Ömür DEMİRER, Hitit University — ORCID [0000-0002-9502-9935](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9502-9935)

## Repository layout

```
kod/            analysis pipeline (Python 3.10); each script's docstring lists its inputs and outputs
kod/calistir/   shell scripts that run the revision batches in the order used for the article
veri/           derived results and small public inputs needed to reproduce the tables and figures
```

Raw input data are **not** redistributed here; `kod/indir_girdiler.py`, `kod/epdk_ulusal_cek.py`
and `kod/faz4_dem_indir.py` download them from their providers. Origin-level result files
(`*_koken.csv`, about 175,000 rows each) are regenerated by the pipeline and are not stored.
Script and column names are in Turkish; the main terms are: *erisim* accessibility, *ilce*
district, *istasyon* station, *soket* socket, *havza* catchment, *kanal* channel, *tikaniklik*
congestion, *enerjik* energy catchment, *koken* origin, *butce* budget, *tohum* random seed.

## Pipeline (run from `kod/`)

1. Inputs: `indir_girdiler.py`, `epdk_ulusal_cek.py`, `faz4_dem_indir.py`; place the TÜİK tables in `veri/` (see `faz0_birlestir.py`).
2. EPDK panels: `epdk_aylik_ayristir.py`, `epdk_soket_doluluk.py`.
3. District layer and stations: `faz0_birlestir.py`, `istasyon_kalite.py`.
4. Road network and origins: `faz1_ag_kur.py`, `faz1_od_matris.py`, `faz1c_izgara_od.py`.
5. Demand and arrival rates: `il_tuketim_tahmin.py`, `turizm_ayristir.py`, `faz2b_lambda_kalibrasyon.py`, `faz2c_huff_kalibrasyon.py`, `faz2c_arz_lambda.py`.
6. Congestion: `faz6_kuyruk_k3.py --pi 1|1.5|3|5|8|12 [--mc 10]`; seasons: `faz8_mevsim_senaryo.py`, then `faz6_kuyruk_k3.py --senaryo YAZ2025|KIS|YAZ2026`.
7. Distance-catchment accessibility: `K3_EK=1 python faz3_erisim_hesapla.py` (adds the 10-minute indicator and the Monte Carlo draws); `X42=1` gives the relocated-station test.
8. Energy-distance: `faz4_yukselti_ornekle.py`, `faz4e_mgm_dogrulama.py`, then for each variant `faz4_enerji_kenar.py [--regen x] [--paux x] [--egim x] [--duz]`, `ENERJI_EK=<suffix> python faz5_kalibre.py`, `ENERJI_EK=<suffix> python faz5_enerji_erisim.py --kalibre`. Fixed-budget winter: `ENERJI_EK=_paux2.2 ENERJI_BUTCE_EK=_paux1.2 python faz5_enerji_erisim.py --kalibre`. Decile transitions: `faz5c_dilim_gecisi.py`.
9. Decomposition: `faz7_shapley.py [--pi 5|8] [--k3 <label>] [--enerji <suffix>]`.
10. District regression and spatial tests: `faz9c_boyutsuz.py`.
11. Investment model (distance catchment): `faz10_katsayi.py`, then `faz11_optimizasyon.py --butce 100e6 --gap 0.0005 --sure 1800 --ek _siki` and the sensitivities in `calistir/milp_duyarlilik.sh`; minimum-cost cover `faz11b_asgari_kapsama.py`. Centre budget shares in Table 9 are recomputed from the saved decisions with `faz11d_merkez_payi.py` (site fixed costs included). Requires Gurobi 12 with a valid licence.
12. Figures: `sekiller_uret.py`.

The revision batches are in `kod/calistir/`: `enerji_zinciri.sh` (all energy variants),
`enerji_ek_varyantlar.sh` (regeneration 0.4, slope limits, fixed-budget winter),
`mc_shapley.sh` (Monte Carlo Shapley ranges), `milp_duyarlilik.sh` (tight-gap Pareto fronts,
cost sensitivities, nine-district target and minimum cover) and `regresyon_ve_kalibrasyon.sh`.
